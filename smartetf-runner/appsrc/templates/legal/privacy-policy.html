Privacy Commitment

Effective Date: December 1, 2024

Last Updated: December 1, 2024

We are committed to protecting your privacy and handling your personal information with the highest level of security and transparency.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Personal Information

We collect information you provide directly to us:

  • Account Information: Name, email address, phone number
  • Payment Information: Billing details (processed securely by payment providers)
  • Communication: Messages, support requests, feedback
  • Preferences: Investment goals, risk tolerance (for educational purposes)

1.2 Usage Information

We automatically collect certain information when you use our service:

  • Technical Data: IP address, browser type, device information
  • Usage Patterns: Pages visited, features used, time spent
  • Performance Data: Educational portfolio tracking (no real money)

1.3 Information We DO NOT Collect

Important: We do NOT collect or have access to:

  • Your broker account passwords or login credentials
  • Your actual investment amounts or real portfolio holdings
  • Your bank account or trading account details
  • Your actual trading activities or transactions

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information for legitimate business purposes:

2.1 Service Delivery

  • Provide educational trading signals and content

  • Send educational market analysis and insights
  • Provide customer support and assistance

2.2 Communication

  • Send service-related notifications
  • Provide educational content and market updates
  • Respond to your inquiries and support requests
  • Send important service announcements

2.3 Business Operations

  • Process payments and manage subscriptions
  • Analyze usage patterns to improve our service
  • Comply with legal and regulatory requirements
  • Protect against fraud and security threats

3. Information Sharing and Disclosure

3.1 We DO NOT Sell Your Data

Clear Commitment: We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties for marketing purposes.

3.2 Limited Sharing

We may share your information only in these specific circumstances:

  • Service Providers: Trusted partners who help deliver our service (payment processors, hosting providers)
  • Legal Requirements: When required by law, court order, or government request
  • Business Transfer: In case of merger, acquisition, or sale of our business
  • Safety Protection: To protect the rights, property, or safety of our users

4. Data Security

We implement comprehensive security measures:

4.1 Technical Safeguards

  • Encryption: All data transmission uses SSL/TLS encryption
  • Access Controls: Strict access controls and authentication
  • Monitoring: Continuous security monitoring and threat detection
  • Regular Updates: Regular security updates and vulnerability assessments

4.2 Administrative Safeguards

  • Employee Training: Regular privacy and security training for staff
  • Access Limitation: Employees access data only on a need-to-know basis
  • Incident Response: Established procedures for security incidents

You have important rights regarding your personal information:

5.1 Access and Control

5.2 Communication Preferences
