Common questions

Quick answers before you start

Do I lose control over trades?

No. You set limits, and you can require approval for amounts above your comfort level.

What if markets don’t crash?

We continue your normal SIP and hold cash buffer. No over-investing when momentum is overheated.

How fast can I start?

Most investors connect broker + configure plan in under 10 minutes. First SIP can run the same day.

Is there a lock-in?

No lock-in. Pause or cancel any time from the dashboard or by disconnecting the broker API key.
