How ETF automation works with your broker

SmartETF connects to your broker for signals, approvals, and execution—while your money stays in your account.

← Back to Home Start SmartETF

Step 1: Secure broker connection

SmartETF uses OAuth or broker APIs (Zerodha, Dhan, Groww, Finvasia) to connect your account. You can revoke access anytime.

  • No fund transfer to SmartETF.
  • Tokens are encrypted and can be rotated.
  • Only your broker executes trades.

Step 2: Rule engine creates orders

The algorithm checks ETF signals, sector caps, and drawdown thresholds. When a signal hits, SmartETF prepares the order for approval.

You set the limits—SIP amount, dip multiplier, and per-ETF caps.

Step 3: Alerts + approvals

Receive WhatsApp/email alerts for every purchase. Approve large orders before they execute.

All transactions are logged with timestamp, ETF, and broker reference.

Step 4: Performance tracking

Track monthly performance, sector exposure, and diversification across Gold BeES, Silver BeES, IT, Infra, Realty, and MAFANG ETFs.
