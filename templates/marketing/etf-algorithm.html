What is ETF algorithm investing?

ETF algorithm investing uses data-driven rules to decide when and how much to invest in ETFs. Instead of guessing, SmartETF follows a predefined framework that looks at volatility, drawdowns, and long-term allocation targets.

You stay in control of the broker account, while the algorithm handles timing, dip-buying, and monthly limits.

Why it works for long-term investors

  • Removes emotional buying and selling.
  • Automates SIPs and increases allocation during dips.
  • Maintains diversification across sectors and asset themes.
  • Tracks performance with clear audit logs.
