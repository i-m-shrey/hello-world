Our Mission

We believe long-term ETF investing should be systematic, transparent, and emotion-free. Our mission is to provide Indian investors with a simple, automated ETF investment system that removes the guesswork from market timing while maintaining complete control over their funds.

How We're Different

SmartETF Algo operates on a simple principle: invest more when markets fall, invest less when markets rise. This rule-based ETF strategy is executed through your own broker account, ensuring you maintain complete ownership and control of your investments.

Our automated ETF investment system provides clear signals for ETF purchases based on predefined market correction thresholds. Every decision is transparent, traceable, and based on historical market patterns - not emotions or speculation.

Why ETF Investing?

ETF investing offers several advantages over traditional mutual funds: lower expense ratios (0.05-0.5% vs 1-2.5%), better transparency, intraday trading flexibility, and no lock-in periods. Combined with our rule-based system, ETF investment becomes a powerful tool for long-term wealth creation.
